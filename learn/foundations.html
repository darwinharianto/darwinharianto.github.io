Vanishing Gradient Insight

Notice how the derivative of Sigmoid peaks at only 0.25 when $x = 0$, and decays asymptotically to 0 whenever $|x| > 2.5$.

Chain Rule In Deep Networks:
$\frac{\partial L}{\partial w_1} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial a_L} \cdot \prod_{l=1}^L \sigma'(z_l) \cdot x$
With 10 layers: $0.25^{10} \approx 9.5 \times 10^{-7}$ (Signal dies!)

ReLU keeps gradient exactly 1.0 for all positive inputs, allowing error signals to flow back across hundreds of layers without attenuation.

GELU Advantage: Smooth probabilistic gating avoids the complete dead-neuron cutoff of ReLU while preserving strong gradients.
