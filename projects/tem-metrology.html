Computer Vision Metrology Stack

At sub-nanometer scale, standard pixel-level thresholding creates step-quantization errors exceeding 20% of total film thickness.

1. Sub-Pixel Parabolic Fitting:

Finds the peak of the image gradient curve $\nabla I(x)$ by fitting a continuous second-order polynomial through the 3 highest gradient pixels: $\delta = \frac{g_{i-1} - g_{i+1}}{2(g_{i-1} - 2g_i + g_{i+1})}$.

2. Normal Vector Raycasting:

Casts 500+ orthogonal interface rays perpendicular to the substrate contour to capture true local thickness distribution without perspective distortion.
