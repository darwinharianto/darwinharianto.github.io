SDXL + LoRA Architecture

Standard text-to-image base models lack knowledge of architectural conventions and CAD drafting standards. By training custom LoRA adapters on architectural floor plans, the model learns realistic spatial proportions, partition boundaries, and functional room adjacencies:

1. SDXL Latent Diffusion Backbone:

Operates in a 128x128 latent space compressed via VAE (8x downsampling), enabling high-fidelity architectural layout generation at 1024x1024 resolution with crisp structural lines.

2. LoRA Fine-Tuning (Rank r=16, α=32):

Trained low-rank decomposition matrices targeting cross-attention projection layers (Wq, Wv) on thousands of curated architectural floor plans. Learns architectural conventions, standard room dimensions, and functional interior arrangements.

3. Seed-Deterministic Latent Sampling:

The initial Gaussian noise latent tensor ε ~ N(0, I) is keyed deterministically by seed number, generating distinct layout typologies (studio, 2LDK, multi-bed suite, penthouse) reliably and repeatably.

02 Architectural Layout Analysis & Verification Pipeline

Automated post-generation algorithmic validation ensuring architectural compliance and habitable circulation.

Room Boundary Segmentation

Extracts wall contours and partition polygons using adaptive thresholding and morphological closing operators in OpenCV to isolate individual room enclosures.

Polygon IoU Matching

Computes geometric Intersection-over-Union between generated wall partitions and architectural target specifications to verify spatial proportion integrity.

Adjacency Graph Check

Constructs a planar graph of room connections (e.g., verifying that bathrooms and bedrooms have proper circulation paths) to prevent nonsensical layouts.
