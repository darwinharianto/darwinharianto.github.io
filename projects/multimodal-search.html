02 Architectural Implementation Details

How vision-language LLM prompting and texture/pattern vector spaces power cross-modal architectural retrieval.

1. LLM Color & Pattern Prompting

The building image is passed to a Vision-Language Model with a structured prompt extracting dominant color hex codes, surface tactile textures, and geometric facade patterns (curtain grids, brick bonds, timber slats).

2. Color & Texture Clustered Vector Space

Instead of coarse categorical tags, the 768D embedding space organizes buildings by visual appearance: cyan glazed grids, terracotta clay bonds, honey cedar slats, and monochrome concrete reliefs.

3. FAISS HNSW Proximity Retrieval

The multimodal text query coordinates navigate the hierarchical small-world proximity graph in sub-30ms, retrieving images sharing the exact aesthetic, color harmony, and facade structural rhythm.
