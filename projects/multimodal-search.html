FAISS HNSW Index Architecture

Traditional exact brute-force search calculates $O(N)$ dot products, taking seconds on million-scale catalogs.

Hierarchical Small World Graphs:

Builds a multi-layer proximity graph where top layers have long-range skip connections (like a skip list), achieving logarithmic search complexity $O(\log N)$.

Gemini VLM Semantic Extraction:

Generates rich descriptors for non-textual attributes (lighting warmth, architectural era, structural layout) into dense multimodal vectors.
