Technical Architecture & Stack

1. Detectron2 + PVNet Head:

Rather than regressing 2D keypoint coordinates directly (fragile to occlusion), PVNet outputs pixel-wise 2D unit vector fields that vote for semantic 3D keypoint centers via RANSAC voting.

2. Camera Calibration & SolvePnP:

Given 3D CAD model vertices $X_i$ and recovered 2D image coordinates $x_i$, solve: $$s \begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} = K \begin{bmatrix} R & t \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} X \\ Y \\ Z \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$$ via Levenberg-Marquardt optimization in OpenCV solvePnP.

3. 3D Synthetic Dataset:

Generated 50k+ synthetic photo-realistic rendering variations using CAD models with domain randomization (lighting, textures, random distractors) for robust physical deployment.

02 Physical Hardware Testing & Production Impact

Heavy Occlusion Resilience
