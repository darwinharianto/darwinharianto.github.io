Hospital Autonomous Robot 6D Pose Estimation via CCTV

Estimated 6-DoF position and orientation ($[X,Y,Z, \text{Roll},\text{Pitch},\text{Yaw}]$) of autonomous mobile robots in hospital hallways from normal overhead ceiling CCTV camera feeds. Leveraged synthetic 3D CAD data, custom vector-field keypoint voting (PVNet), intrinsic/extrinsic camera calibration ($K, [R|t]$), and SolvePnP to achieve < 5.0 cm localization accuracy.

Technical Architecture & Stack

1. Detectron2 + Custom PVNet Head:

Predicts semantic segmentation masks and pixel-wise 2D unit vector fields that vote for the 8 corner vertices of the cubic AGV via RANSAC voting, maintaining robustness against obstacle occlusions in busy hospital corridors.

2. Camera Calibration & SolvePnP:

Using intrinsic camera parameters ($f_x, f_y, c_x, c_y$) from CCTV calibration and 2D-3D keypoint correspondences, recovers 6-DoF rigid transformation $[R|t]$ via Levenberg-Marquardt PnP optimization:

s · [u, v, 1]ᵀ = K · [R | t] · [X, Y, Z, 1]ᵀ
3. Synthetic CAD Dataset:

Synthesized 50k+ hospital hallway scenes featuring varying lighting reflections on linoleum floors, shadows, patient gurneys, and camera angles to train PVNet without physical robot manual labeling.

4. Real-world Verification:

Tested on live physical AGVs operating under real hospital lighting. Exceeded performance spec by reducing position error to < 5 cm (achieved ~2.7 cm).

02 Hardware Deployment & Fleet Impact

Validated on mobile robotics fleets across clinical hallway environments.

Sub-5cm Precision Spec

Achieved average 3D translation error of 2.7 cm and orientation error under 1.5°, enabling precise automatic door triggering and elevator docking.

38+ FPS Real-Time Inference

Optimized the PVNet voting stage with vectorized CUDA kernels and TensorRT INT8 quantization, accelerating inference from 14 FPS to 38 FPS.

Occlusion Resilience

Because every pixel votes for keypoint locations via direction vectors, the robot's pose was reliably estimated even when pedestrians or medical carts occluded 40% of the body.
